Dofus Economy

Understand your crafting and sales.

A private economy tracker for player-observed Dofus Touch prices, recipes, crafting costs, and sales activity.

What the application does

  • Search the item catalog and review crafting recipes.
  • Record observed market prices while preserving price history.
  • Calculate ingredient costs, potential crafting profit, and return on investment.
  • Track active listings, completed sales, and historical sales results.

Private access with Google sign-in

Anyone may sign in with Google to request access. The owner approves accounts before they can use the tracker. Approved accounts share item catalogs, recipes, icons, and market prices within each game/server. Listings, sales history, profits, and personal reports belong to each account. Google sign-in requests basic profile and email information to authenticate the account; it does not request access to a Gmail inbox.

Read the privacy policy for details about authentication, cookies, data use, and privacy requests.

Public source, private records

The project source code is public. The published repository contains code, documentation, and synthetic examples. Credentials and operational databases are kept separately.
