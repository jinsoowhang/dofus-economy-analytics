Privacy policy

Dofus Economy is a private, owner-operated tracker for observed item prices, recipes, crafting economics, and sales activity.

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Google sign-in information

Google handles account sign-in. The authentication gateway requests the openid, email, and profile scopes. Google may provide an account identifier, verified email address, name, and profile picture. This information is used to verify identity, maintain an authenticated session, and associate private economy records with your account and enforce account approval. Your stable Google account identifier and email address are stored privately in the account registry.

The application does not request access to Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, or calendars. Google passwords are entered on Google's sign-in service and are not collected by this application.

Sessions and storage

The authentication gateway stores an encrypted, signed session in a secure browser cookie. The session may contain Google tokens needed to validate and refresh sign-in. The configured session expires after 12 hours and may refresh while in use. Authentication tokens are not forwarded to the economy application or stored in its catalog, pricing, or sales tables.

Authentication credentials are stored in private server configuration. A first sign-in saves an access request and sends your email address to the operator for approval. Request and delivery status are stored privately. Browser-local storage remembers account-separated tracker preferences, recipe selections, and checklist progress. These preferences can be cleared through browser settings.

Operational records and service diagnostics

The tracker stores catalogs, recipes, shared market prices, and private account-owned listings and sales. Approved users can contribute market prices, including prices from their listings, relistings, and completed sales. Item, price, and observation time are shared within that game/server; other accounts cannot browse your listings, sales history, profits, or personal reports. An internal contributor key supports corrections without publishing your email or Google identifier. Free-text price notes are visible only to their contributor and the operator. The administrator interface manages account access and does not provide other users’ sales reports. The existing manual BigQuery publication includes only the original owner’s Sales history and anonymous shared market prices; other users’ sales, personal notes, account identities, and authentication credentials are excluded.

Limited service diagnostics may contain technical information such as connection metadata, timestamps, and error details. They support operation and troubleshooting and are subject to log rotation. Diagnostic records and backups remain private.

Use and sharing

Google account information is used for authentication and access control. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models. The website does not add advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.

The operator and the infrastructure providers needed to host, secure, authenticate, and back up the service may process the information required for those functions. Google processes sign-in information under its own privacy policy. Public source code and synthetic examples do not include private operational records or authentication credentials.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can sign out, clear browser cookies and local storage, and remove the application's access through your Google account connections. For questions, access removal, or deletion requests, contact the operator using the support email displayed on the Google consent screen for this application.

Revoking an account’s approval does not automatically erase operational price or sales history. The operator handles deletion requests separately, including any applicable private backups and analytics snapshots. This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change.
